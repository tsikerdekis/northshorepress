# Delete your Bloomstead account

This page explains how to delete your **Bloomstead** account and the data associated with it.

Bloomstead is published by **Northshore Press LLC**. The steps below apply to the Bloomstead
game on Android, on the web, and on desktop.

---

## Delete your account from inside the game (recommended)

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

1. Open **Bloomstead** and sign in.
2. From the main menu, open the **account** menu (the account button, top right).
3. Under *Delete my account*, type your password to confirm.
4. Press **Really delete everything?** to confirm a second time.

Your account is deleted straight away. You do not need to contact us, and there is no waiting
period.

## If you cannot sign in

If you no longer have access to the game or your password, email
[support@northshorepress.co](mailto:support@northshorepress.co) **from an address you can receive
mail at**, and tell us the username of the account you want deleted. We will verify the request and
delete the account, normally within 30 days.

---

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your **username** and **password hash**
- Your **star count** and your place on the **leaderboard**
- Your **friend list** and any pending **friend requests**
- Your **account record** itself, including the platform it was created on and any Play Games
  identifier linked to it
- Your **saved "remember me" login token**

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Purchase records** are retained as long as required for tax and accounting obligations. These
  do not identify you personally in our systems beyond the purchase itself.
- **Advertising data** is held by Google (AdMob) under its own policies, not by us. You can reset
  your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
- **Aggregated, non-identifying** service logs may persist for a short period for security and
  abuse prevention, and are not linked back to a deleted account.

## Deleting some data without deleting the account

You can change or remove social data at any time without deleting your account:

- **Remove a friend**: open the friends screen and remove them; the friendship disappears for both
  of you.
- **Change your password**: account menu → *Change password*. This also signs out every device.
- **Clear your saved login**: log out, which revokes the "remember me" token on your device.

We do not keep any other personal data about you, so there is nothing else to remove.
