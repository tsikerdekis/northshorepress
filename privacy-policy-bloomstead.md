# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

Northshore Press LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game **"Bloomstead"** (the "Game") on Android, the web, or desktop.

Bloomstead is a multiplayer gardening card game. To play with other people you create an account, and that account is what we store. The Game is supported by **advertising** and offers optional **in-app purchases**; both are described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you give us

- **Username:** 3–20 letters, numbers or underscores, which you choose.
- **Password:** stored only as a salted **scrypt hash**. We never store, and cannot recover, your actual password.
- **Play Games identity (Android, optional):** if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games instead of a password, we store the Play Games player identifier that Google provides so that we can recognise you next time.

### Information created by playing

- **Star count and leaderboard position:** the stars you earn by winning rounds.
- **Friend list:** the players you have added as friends, and any pending friend requests. Both sides of a friendship are stored.
- **Platform:** whether the account was created on Android, the web or desktop. A name only has to be unique on the platform it was chosen on, so this is needed to tell two players with the same name apart.
- **Account timestamps:** when the account was created and when it was last used.

### Information collected by advertising and billing partners

- **Advertising data (via Google AdMob):** the advertising identifier (Advertising ID) of your device, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions such as impressions and clicks.
- **Purchase information (via Google Play Billing):** the product you bought, the purchase token and transaction status. **We never see or store your card number or billing address** - payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

### Information we process but do not store

- **IP address:** when you connect, our game server reads your connecting address and passes it to our account service so that rate limits and brute-force protection apply per player rather than per server. It is used only for those checks and is **not written to our database**.
- **Gameplay:** the gardens, cards, positions and round scores exist only in memory while a round is running. They are discarded when the round ends and are not stored.

### What we do NOT collect

We do **not** collect your email address, real name, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location. The Android build requests Internet access, network-state access (so advertising can tell when you are offline) and the advertising identifier permission.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game keeps a small amount of data on your own device, which never leaves it unless you are signed in:

- **Settings:** sound and music volume, graphics and camera preferences.
- **Remember me (optional):** if you tick "Remember me for 30 days", an encrypted login token is saved so you do not have to retype your password. It contains your username and a rotating token, **never your password**, and it expires 30 days after the login that created it. The encryption key is derived from a device identifier, which is used locally for this purpose only and is never transmitted to us. Logging out, changing your password, or deleting your account removes it.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to:

- Create and secure your account, and sign you in
- Run multiplayer matches and record the stars you earn
- Show the leaderboard and your friends
- Prevent cheating, abuse and brute-force password guessing
- **Show advertising**, and measure whether it was seen (see section 5)
- **Process in-app purchases** and deliver what you bought (see section 5)
- Operate, maintain and debug the service

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your account data (username, password, stars, friends) for advertising or profiling.

## 4. What Other Players Can See

Bloomstead is multiplayer, so some of your information is visible to other players by design:

- Your **username** and **star count** appear on the public leaderboard if you have at least one star.
- Your **username** and **online status** are visible to players on your friend list.
- Your **username** appears to the other players in a match you are in.

Your password, and any hashed form of it, is never shown to anyone.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We do **not** sell your information, and we do not share your account data (username, password, stars, friends) with advertisers or data brokers.

The Game does use the following third-party services, which process data to provide their function:

### Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's **advertising identifier** and approximate location derived from your IP address to serve and measure ads. Where you have consented, ads may be personalised; otherwise they are non-personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your device settings (Android: **Settings → Google → Ads**). See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised advertising and you can change or withdraw it at any time from the consent prompt in the Game.

### Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

In-app purchases are processed by **Google Play**, not by us. Google handles your payment details; we receive only confirmation that a purchase succeeded and an identifier for it, which we use to deliver the item and to restore your purchases. Google's handling of that data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Google Play Games (Android, only if you sign in with it)

Verifies who you are. Google's handling of that data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Cloud hosting providers

Run our game servers and account service on our behalf. They process data only to provide that infrastructure.

Bloomstead contains **no analytics SDKs and no third-party crash-reporting SDKs**.

## 6. Where Your Data Is Stored

Account and star data are stored on servers we operate, located in the **United States**. If you play from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

## 7. How Long We Keep It

- **Account data** (username, password hash, stars, friends, timestamps) is kept until you delete your account.
- **Login tokens** expire automatically within 30 days.
- **Gameplay data** is not retained at all.
- **Purchase records** are kept as long as needed to honour your purchase, to restore it if you reinstall, and to meet tax and accounting obligations.
- **Advertising data** is retained by Google according to its own policies; we do not store it ourselves.

## 8. Deleting Your Account and Your Data

You can delete your account from inside the Game at any time: open the account menu and choose **"Delete my account"**. You will be asked to confirm with your password.

Deleting your account **permanently and immediately** removes your account, your stars, your leaderboard entry, and your friendships. This cannot be undone. Note that a deleted account cannot be restored, so any stars you earned are lost.

Deleting your account does **not** cancel or refund in-app purchases, which are handled by Google Play - see [Google Play refunds](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). To control personalised advertising, use the consent prompt in the Game or your device's advertising settings.

If you have signed in with Play Games and no longer have access to the Game, you can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Bloomstead is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because Bloomstead accounts hold very little data, most of this is available directly in the Game:

- **Access and export:** the account menu shows your username and star count; your friend list is shown in the friends screen.
- **Correction:** you can change your password at any time in the account menu.
- **Deletion:** see section 8.
- **Advertising choices:** reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your device settings, or use the consent prompt in the Game.

For any other request, contact us at the address below.

## 11. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and compared in constant time. Traffic to our servers is encrypted in transit, and access tokens are short-lived. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If the changes are significant, we will provide notice within the Game or on this website. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
